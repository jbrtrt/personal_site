03 — Board

Seven modules on one board.

  1. M1 · ViveSense, Inc.

    Fertility screening that never leaves the house

    Male-factor causes roughly half of infertility, and the diagnostic path starts with a clinic visit most men never book. ViveSense is core hardware for an at-home semen analysis — optical, patent-backed, built to be read at a kitchen table rather than a bench.

    Hardware · optics · US20230204935A1 · NSF SBIR I · I-Corps

    In development. Screening and monitoring — not a diagnosis.

  2. M2 · NEPHRA ONE

    The dialysis chair, deleted

    A concept implantable total artificial kidney: continuous filtration in place of three sessions a week and the hundred and sixty-eight hour week that dialysis quietly takes from people. It is the direct descendant of the ambulatory hemofiltration work I did in the Artificial Organs Lab.

    Implantable · hemofiltration · ASAIO 2020 · investigational

  3. M3 · stoneidx

    A comorbidity index for kidney stones

    Five independent arms fit across national discharge data, then harmonised into one integer point table. Firth penalised regression where the data separates, tetrachoric correlation written from scratch, factor count chosen by parallel analysis rather than an eigenvalue rule of thumb. The unit of analysis is the encounter, never the person — enforced by a lint test that fails the build if a source file says otherwise.

    HCUP NIS / NEDS / NASS / NRD · Python · cluster-robust

    Modest AUC is the expected result. Nothing here is tuned toward a number.

  4. M4 · Ocula Health, Inc.

    The other 8,758 hours

    Twenty minutes in March, then a hundred and ninety-two days of dinners, doses and decisions made alone. Ocula is a coach that lives in those hours and sends the care team a short summary afterwards. Who sees what is not a settings page three menus deep — it is the architecture. Summaries travel. Raw life stays home.

    Longitudinal care · on-device capture · consent-gated egress

  5. M5 · FlopCheck

    Did the contact actually do that?

    Three-dimensional pose off broadcast video, then an impulse-momentum check against the motion that followed: was the reaction consistent with the contact, or embellished? Built out of my own impact-detection patent. The honest part is the limitation — monocular depth, not the pose model, is the accuracy ceiling, and the usual benchmarks are root-relative, so they hide exactly the quantity the physics consumes.

    Inverse dynamics · Monte Carlo envelope · US12511900B2

  6. M6 · Lantern

    A steady presence for the hours between visits

    A bedside companion for people living with serious illness, for the long quiet stretches when no one is in the room. The safety layer is deterministic and runs on every utterance independently of the model; inferred memory is never voiced as fact; prognosis, diagnosis and dosing return refusals rather than answers; and a human is one touch away from every screen. It is the only thing I have built where the correct behaviour is usually to say less.

    Palliative · deterministic safety layer · provenance-tagged memory

  7. M7 · notes2anki

    Flashcards that grade themselves, then argue back

    Medical notes carry meaning in their formatting — highlight colour, indentation, an arrow, an odd capitalisation. The pipeline reads that structure, writes cards against a real study standard, then judges them on whether they actually test understanding. Weak cards get one rewrite and are otherwise dropped. The prompts that produce them are revised and adopted only after a blind A/B gate says the new version is better.

    Python stdlib · two-tier audit · blind A/B adoption gate
